## Notes

Yellow jewels come from achievements (each pays a few, e.g. "Jewel Collector" ×10), the max-level daily, weekly and monthly quests, the monthly War of Warmonger ranking, trading and the gacha ([[gameplay/progression-and-economy|Progression and economy]] §2–3, §7, *guide + image*; [[gameplay/events-and-schedules|Events and schedules]] §2, *notes*). 1 jewel = 1,000 gold on the auction house; a yellow jewel is trade-locked 24 h after receipt (WM 0412) ([[gameplay/progression-and-economy|Progression and economy]] §3, [[gameplay/events-and-schedules|Events and schedules]] §9).
