# Events, schedules and PvP rewards

The timed events, PvP rewards, fort-siege values and buff systems, with their numbers, from the official Warmonger (**WM**, 2018–19) and Crush Online (**CO**, 2016–17) Steam announcements. Schedule and reward tables come from the patch-note images and are laid out in our own form. All times are **server time** unless marked; the notes say `/time` showed the server clock ([WM 0712][wm0712]). Broader context is in [[gameplay/pvp-and-matches|PvP and matches]] and [[gameplay/patch-history|Patch notes and other sources]].

Tags: **notes** · **image** · **client** · **guess**.

## 1. Daily event schedule

Three versions:

1. **3 May 2018**: War of Warmonger every 3 h; Holy Thing twice a day ([WM 0503][wm0503]). Holy Gift was already announced daily at 19:00 ([WM 0420][wm0420]). *notes*
2. **12 Jul 2018** ([WM 0712][wm0712], linked schedule image `979fadc9…png`), the same every day of the week:

| Hour | Event |
|---|---|
| 02, 08, 14, 20 | War of Warmonger (Sunday 20:00 = **Civil War** instead) |
| 04, 06, 10, 12, 16, 18, 22, 24 | Battle Arena |
| 07, 19 | Holy Thing |

3. **7 Nov 2018** ([WM 1107][wm1107], linked schedule image `94fe7626…png`), the same every day:

| Hour | Event |
|---|---|
| 00, 02, 04, 08, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 22 | War of Warmonger (Sunday 20:00 = **Civil War** instead) |
| 06, 18 | Battle Arena |
| 07, 19 | Holy Things |

- Event warning shown **5 min** before the start (was 2.5 min) ([WM 0712][wm0712]). *notes*
- Since [WM 1107][wm1107], War of Warmonger fills both teams with **up to 15 bots** and its nexus has **70,560 HP**. Bot count scales with how long the battlefield has been running ([WM 0824][wm0824]). Kills and assists on bots give fewer medal points ([WM 0920][wm0920]). From [WM 0124][wm0124], bot kills count for the daily Kill Player quest. *notes*
- Battle Arena: **max 5 per team** ([WM 0719][wm0719]); rules like mock battles ([WM 0420][wm0420]). *notes*

## 4. Double Event Field

[WM 0628][wm0628] *notes*:

- Shown only to the **weaker nation**; spawns on a land near that nation's border.
- A new one **every hour**, never more than one active. If the current one is under attack when the timer runs out, the next spawn waits for that fight to end.
- If the weaker nation takes it, its players get **double medal rewards**.

## 6. Shaia Blessing (point buff)

From [WM 0809][wm0809] (text + image 2), changed in [WM 0817][wm0817] and [WM 1107][wm1107]. *notes + image*

- Point pool, **max 10,000**. The buff is active while the player has points.
- Gaining points: a starting amount on account creation; time spent in a village, fortress, castle or training camp; offline for 30+ min; the **Blessing of Shaia** item.
  - Offline/town gain is capped at **2,000** (→ **1,000** from [WM 1107][wm1107]) and grows with time: 5 min = 25 points, 400 min = 2,000 (5 points per minute).
  - Item: **1,000** points (→ **2,000** from 1107). Item price 3,000 → **1,500** ([WM 0817][wm0817]; currency not stated, jewels *guess*).
- Spending points: each normal monster kill **5** (→ **10** from 1107), each boss kill **100**. In Gaia field wars the cost depends on fame and medals earned.

| Tier | Points | War | Hunt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Normal | 1–2,000 | "100% fame and 100% medal points" | monster EXP and drop rate +20% |
| Gold | 2,001–10,000 | "130% fame and 130% medal points" | monster EXP and drop rate +30% |

### Shaia Legion donations

[WM 1128][wm1128] (text + image 2). Each player may donate **200,000 gold per day** to the legion. Paying **100 jewels** buys one extra donation that day (no limit). The legion reward lasts one week, every member gets it, and donations count towards the next week while the current one runs. *notes + image*

| Tier | Donation total (gold) | Shaia increase | Guild core unlocked | Core durability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3,000,000 | 10% | – | – |
| 2 | 6,000,000 | 10% | Recovery Shot | 50 |
| 3 | 10,000,000 | 10% | Fire Support | 50 |
| 4 | 15,000,000 | 20% | Reinforce Nexus | 100 |

| 6 | 35,000,000 | 20% | Powerful Remote Bomb | 50 |

[WM 1107][wm1107] added two legion cores that enable TP skills on the land where they are placed: **Powerful Remote Bomb** (PvP: hits the nexus after destroying all enemy attack towers; PvE: hits all bosses after the middle one) and **Powerful Nexus Remote Bomb** (a stronger nexus bomb). *notes*. The client `Skill_TP` has extra rows (ids 14–17: 10,000 TP / 180 s, 2,500 / 240 s, 3,000 / 300 s, 2,500 / 240 s) outside the always-available set; these are probably the core-unlocked skills. *client + guess*

## 8. Jungle and land-war timers

- Each battle lasts **40 min**. Troll and Ogre first appear at **36:00** and respawn **4 min** after dying; the Bear appears at **35:00** and respawns **5 min** after dying ([blog pvp][blog-pvp], same as the definitive guide). *guide*
- From [WM 0830][wm0830] the **medium jungle mob** spawns as soon as PvP starts. [WM 1018][wm1018] changed jungle spawn times and effects again, with no numbers given. Giant Bears got a little faster ([WM 0412][wm0412]). *notes*
- TP per kill per the blog: normal 30, elite 100, **Ogre/Bear/Troll 1,500 each**; tower 500 TP. The definitive and strategy guides give Ogre 2,500 and Bear 3,000, so the jungle values changed over time or the blog simplified. *guide*
- Crush Online: holding a tower gave **8 TP per tower every 10 s** ([CO 1123][co1123]). *notes*

## 11. Crush Online (2016–17) numbers

From the CO Steam announcements. Legacy, for reference. *notes*

- Season 1 ranks ([CO 1123][co1123]): 1 King; 20 Marshals (8 after the next council); 3 Imperators (individual rank 1–3); 20 Generals (4–25); 50 Officers (25–73). Channels cut from 5 to 3.
- Life Saviour: unusable in the battle arena, **120 s** cooldown. Premium Life Saviour (bundle of 25 for 1,000 jewels in the auction house): heals 50% max HP. Fame Life Saviour (1,000 fame at the merits merchant): heals 30% max HP. Client items 1801/1802 (1802 fame price 1,000) *client*.
- Invincible Armor / Gloves of Ghost effects: self only, **3 s**.
- Auction house prices: High Reinforcing Adjuvants 2,000 jewels / 10,000,000 gold; VIP 30 days 2,500 jewels / 12,500,000 gold; Constructing Fort 4,500 jewels.
- Lords of the Land buff steps (1123): 1 Attack and AP +10; 2 cooldown and respawn time −5%; 3 SP gain +6; 4 Attack and AP +4%; 5 heal and mana regen +20 and doubles the whole buff. Since [CO 1222][co1222] it is earned by destroying the enemy nexus (PvP) or killing the land boss (PvE), and lost by fleeing or losing.
- Beginner channel ([CO 1107][co1107]): below Lv 25; only grade D/C weapons and artifacts in war; dungeons up to Lv 3; more legion fame for defending forts there.
- [CO 1222][co1222]: TP and SP penalty grows with distance from the nation fort; basic VIPs get 5 daily jewels; the Civil War stake is not refunded; fort taxes collectable until Sunday 24:00 CET and paid out as legion ether; Rank 1–2 skill stones sold for gold; costume shop prices 2,000–3,800 jewels for 14 days (Christmas set +10% drop); permanent costume 10,000,000 gold; Costume Remover 425,000 gold.
- Season 2 quests could pay up to **13,000 jewels a month** ([CO 0202][co0202]); 4,500 free jewels given to all accounts on 23 Nov 2016 ([CO 1125][co1125]).
