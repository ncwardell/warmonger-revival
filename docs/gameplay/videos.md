# Videos

Gameplay videos of Warmonger (2018–2019) and Crush Online (2016–2017), grouped by what they show, most useful first. Timestamps link straight to the moment. Crush Online footage is the older game: same world and systems, earlier numbers.

Use them to recover what the client cannot tell us: where NPCs stand, quest text and order, shop stock and prices, monster spawns, timers and UI values. When you take a fact from a video, cite it with its timestamp (see [[wiki-style]]).

> [!tip] Rebuilding the tutorial
> Start with the first three videos under *Tutorial*: they cover character creation, the tutorial island and the first quests in order. The "First steps lvl 1–17" video linked from the Steam definitive guide would have been the best source, but it is now private.
